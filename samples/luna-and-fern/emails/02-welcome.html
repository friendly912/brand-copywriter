Welcome to the slow side of skincare

Hi there,

I started Luna & Fern at my kitchen table in 2019, after one too many products that promised the world and left my skin red and angry.

What I wanted was simple: fewer ingredients, ones you can actually pronounce, that feel lovely to use twice a day. That's still all we make.

Three things to know about us

  • Gentle first. Every formula is tested on sensitive skin before it's tested on anyone else.
  • Small batches. We make everything in Portland, a few hundred jars at a time.
  • Real people answer. Reply to any email and it lands in our inbox, not a ticket queue.
