## A small confession

For years I told you to use serum *after* toner. Then our chemist, Priya, gently pointed out that with our formulas the order barely matters. So: do what feels nice. Your skin won't hold it against you.

## Your questions, answered

**"Can I use Dew Serum under makeup?"** Yes. Give it a minute to sink in first.

**"Is the Cloud Cleanser okay for eyes?"** Around them, yes. In them, please don't.
