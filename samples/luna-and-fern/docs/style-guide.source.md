# Luna & Fern — Voice & Tone Guide

## Who we are when we write
We write like Mia talks: a warm, unhurried friend who happens to know a lot about skin. Calm, honest, a little playful. Never clinical, never pushy.

## Principles
1. **Gentle over loud.** We invite, we don't urge. No countdown panic, no "LAST CHANCE!!!".
2. **Honest over hype.** We never promise to erase, reverse or fix. Skin is skin. We say what a product does and how it feels.
3. **Specific over vague.** "Leaves your skin soft, never squeaky" beats "amazing results".
4. **Personal over corporate.** First person from Mia ("I"), "we" for the studio team. Always "you", never "customers".

## Mechanics
- Sentence case everywhere, including subject lines and headlines.
- Short paragraphs: one to three sentences.
- Em dashes are welcome. Exclamation points: at most one per email, and never in a subject line.
- Emoji: rarely. A single 🌿 is fine in a newsletter; never in promos.
- Open with "Hi love," (or "Hi there," for new subscribers). Sign off "Love, Mia".
- Prices are written plainly: $36, not $36.00.

## Words we love
ritual, gentle, calm, soft, lovely, slow, small batch, your skin

## Words we never use
anti-aging, flawless, miracle, cheap, perfect skin, flaws, fix, hurry, act now

## Subject lines
Under 45 characters. Warm and curious, often a gentle invitation or a small promise. No ALL CAPS, no emoji, no exclamation points.

## CTAs
Soft and specific, three or four words: "Build your ritual", "Find your first ritual", "Say goodnight to dry skin". Never "Buy now" or "Shop now!".
