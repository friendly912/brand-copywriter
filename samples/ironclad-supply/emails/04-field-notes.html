The jacket that went to Alaska

Dan Kowalski fishes out of Kodiak. Three winters ago he bought a Ridge Jacket.

Last month he sent it back to us. Not to return it. To get the cuffs replaced.

"Everything else is fine," he wrote. "The cuffs just got chewed up by the lines."

We replaced them. Free. That's what the lifetime repair program is for.

From the shop floor

We're testing a waxed version of the Chore Coat. Early verdict: sheds rain, smells like a hardware store. We mean that as a compliment.

Tip of the month

Canvas stiff out of the box? Wash it cold, hang it dry, wear it hard. Two weeks and it'll fit like it's yours.
