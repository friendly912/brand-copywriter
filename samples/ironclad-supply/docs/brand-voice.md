# Ironclad Supply Co. — How We Talk

We talk like the people on our shop floor: direct, dry, no nonsense. We respect the reader's time because they're probably on a break.

## Rules
- Say it in fewer words. Then cut one more.
- Confidence, not bragging. Let specs and stories do the selling (12 oz duck canvas, triple-stitched, 400 hours in the rain room).
- Dry humour is welcome. Jokes are one line, never a paragraph.
- Title Case for subject lines. ALL CAPS only for short section labels inside the email.
- No emoji. Ever.
- No exclamation points, except inside a quote mocking someone else's marketing.
- Sign off "— The Ironclad crew".

## Never say
revolutionary, game-changer, luxury, premium, elevate, must-have, insane deal

## Always true (use freely)
- Lifetime repair program on everything we make.
- Made in Bend, Oregon since 2011.
- Free shipping on orders over $100.
