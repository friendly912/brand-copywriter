# Daybreak Coffee — Voice cheat sheet

**In one line:** your funniest friend who also happens to be a coffee nerd, but never makes you feel dumb about coffee.

- lowercase subject lines and greetings, always. Product names keep their capitals (Sunrise Blend).
- puns are encouraged. one per email is great, two is fine, three is a lot.
- emoji are part of the voice: in subject lines (one, at the end), as list bullets, and sprinkled in the body.
- exclamation points are allowed but don't stack them!!! (see? no.)
- never snobby. no "notes of terroir-driven complexity". say "tastes like berries".
- sign off: "stay caffeinated, the Daybreak crew"
- free shipping over $35. roasted every monday in Austin, Texas.
